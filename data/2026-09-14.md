### [1] [Physics Based Triple Debye Dielectric Modeling and Multi Layer ADE FDTD Simulation of Terahertz Pulse Reflection for Breast Cancer Detection](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.12701)
*Ali Asghar Molavi Choobini,Mehran Shahmansouri*

Main category: physics.comp-ph

TL;DR: 本文开发了一种基于三德拜（triple Debye）介电模型的物理框架，用于定量预测宽带太赫兹（THz）与人体乳腺组织的相互作用。通过对离体人乳腺组织的THz时域光谱测量数据进行非线性最小二乘拟合，验证了该模型相比单德拜和双德拜模型具有更高的拟合精度。FDTD仿真表明恶性组织因含水量更高表现出增强的反射振幅、延迟的脉冲到达和波形展宽，且偏振相关的伪布鲁斯特角可提供额外的对比机制。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 乳腺癌是全球高发癌症，早期准确检测意义重大。现有生物组织介电描述主要基于单德拜或双德拜模型，无法捕捉多尺度弛豫动力学，限制了THz反射脉冲的定量解释能力。THz成像对组织水合状态敏感，但缺乏能够准确描述宽带THz色散和吸收的物理模型。

Method: 1. 构建三德拜介电框架，明确纳入三个物理上不同的弛豫过程：自由水旋转动力学、结合水弛豫（τ₂）和超快界面/大分子极化（τ₃）；2. 通过非线性最小二乘拟合从已发表的离体人乳腺组织THz时域光谱实验数据中提取模型参数；3. 采用全波FDTD ADE方法模拟真实多层乳腺结构；4. 进行偏振和角度分辨的菲涅尔分析。

Result: 三德拜模型将拟合均方根误差从单德拜的0.7773和双德拜的0.2976显著降低至0.0199。FDTD仿真显示恶性组织因水合程度更高，其反射THz脉冲表现出：反射振幅增加、脉冲到达延迟、波形展宽增强。偏振分析发现水合依赖的伪布鲁斯特角可选择性抑制低水合的正常组织和脂肪组织反射，提供额外对比机制。

Conclusion: 三德拜介电模型能够准确描述宽带THz-组织相互作用，可实现组织水合状态的定量评估。反射脉冲的时间特征和伪布鲁斯特角效应为乳腺癌THz成像提供了有价值的物理对比机制，有望推动THz技术在乳腺癌临床诊断中的应用。

Abstract: Terahertz (THz) imaging has emerged as a promising non-ionizing modality for breast cancer assessment owing to its intrinsic sensitivity to tissue hydration. However, existing dielectric descriptions of biological tissue, largely restricted to single- or double-Debye models, fail to capture the multiscale relaxation dynamics governing broadband THz dispersion and absorption, thereby limiting the quantitative interpretation of reflected pulse signatures.Here, a physics based triple Debye dielectric framework is developed to quantitatively predict broadband THz-tissue interactions. The proposed model explicitly incorporates three physically distinct relaxation processes associated with free-water rotational dynamics, bound-water relaxation (τ_2), and ultrafast interfacial/macromolecular polarization (τ_3). Model parameters are obtained by nonlinear least-squares fitting to experimentally measured refractive-index data digitized from published THz time-domain spectroscopy measurements of ex vivo human breast tissue. Compared with conventional Debye formulations, the proposed model substantially improves the fitting accuracy, reducing the root-mean-square error from 0.7773 (single Debye) and 0.2976 (double Debye) to only 0.0199 for the triple-Debye model. Fullwave FDTD ADE simulations of realistic multilayer breast structures further demonstrate that reflected THz pulses encode tissue hydration through reproducible temporal signatures, including increased reflection amplitude, delayed pulse arrival, and enhanced waveform broadening in malignant tissue. Polarization and angle resolved Fresnel analysis further indicates that hydration-dependent pseudo-Brewster minima provide an additional contrast mechanism by selectively suppressing reflections from low hydration normal and adipose tissues.

### [2] [Crystallographic and Electronic Phase Changes in TiTe2 via Atmospheric and Electron Beam Exposure](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.11992)
*Bishal Pokhrel,Joel Quarnstrom,Saraswati Shrestha,Halle Helfrich,Elena Echeverria,David N. McIlroy,Andrew J. Yost*

Main category: cond-mat.mtrl-sci

TL;DR: 研究高压制备的TiTe2材料的表面敏感性，发现空气暴露导致表面金属-半导体转变及晶相转变


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 研究过渡金属硫族化物的表面敏感性，特别是TiTe2在空气暴露和电子束辐照下的表面稳定性和结构变化

Method: 采用化学气相传输法(CVT)高压生长TiTe2，利用STM/STS、XRD和TEM等技术表征机械剥离样品的表面态和晶体结构

Result: 发现空气暴露后表面发生金属-半导体转变，表面粗糙度增加表明形成吸附层；晶体结构从单晶六方相经畸变单斜相转变为多晶；可能形成非化学计量比的TixTey超晶格

Conclusion: TiTe2表面对空气暴露高度敏感，存在表面氧化/分解现象，导致电子性质和晶体结构发生显著变化

Abstract: In this study, we examine the surface sensitivity of a transition metal dichalcogenide (TiTe2) grown using the Chemical Vapor Transport (CVT) technique at high pressure and study the surface changes in the sample upon exposure to air as well as its crystallographic properties upon e-beam exposure. We examine the local density of States (LDOS) of the mechanically exfoliated sample surface before and after exposure using Scanning Tunneling Microscopy/Spectroscopy (STM/STS) and find a metal-to-semiconductor transition at the surface. The STM analysis shows a clear change in surface roughness which indicates the formation of an adlayer on the surface of the sample. The crystal structure was examined using X-ray Diffraction (XRD) and Transmission Electron Microscopy (TEM), indicating a phase transition from a single-crystalline hexagonal phase to a polycrystalline state via a distorted monoclinic phase. Furthermore, the presence of a possible superlattice suggests non-stoichiometric TixTey at the surface of the exposed layer.
