Motivation: Ga2O3器件的长期高温运行是实现该技术广泛应用的关键障碍，但目前相关研究严重不足。现有演示表明器件受限于材料或掺杂剂不稳定导致的性能随时间退化。

Method: 制备Ga2O3基氢传感器（Pt肖特基二极管和Cr2O3/Ga2O3 p-n二极管），在600°C下进行800-1800小时长期稳定性测试，采用N2和低浓度H2（500-1500 ppm）循环暴露。运用瞬态电流密度（在-0.1 V下测量）和周期性J-V特性表征跟踪性能，采用热发射和Lambert W模型分析，使用TEM和TOF-SIMS进行失效分析。

Result: 尽管传感器信号和灵敏度逐渐下降，器件在数周运行期间仍能区分不同氢浓度。降解行为与器件架构密切相关：Cr2O3:Mg逐渐劣化，与已知的Mg迁移一致；Pt肖特基二极管在1000小时后显示剧烈变化；Cr2O3:N初始性能最低但最稳定，800小时失效。热发射和Lambert W建模证实氢暴露通过质子诱导偶极子机制降低界面势垒，这是两种二极管的共同机制。老化Pt肖特基二极管的TEM显示Pt晶粒生长和微孔形成是主要降解机制。TOF-SIMS确认氮掺杂剂仍局限于Cr2O3:N层，支持N掺杂作为比Mg掺杂更稳定但性能较低的替代方案。

Conclusion: N掺杂是实现Ga2O3基氢传感器稳定高温运行的可行方案，尽管性能低于Mg掺杂。Pt肖特基二极管虽初始性能好但长期稳定性差，需要进一步优化。Mg掺杂虽然性能较高但存在迁移问题。未来需要开发新的器件结构或封装方法来克服高温下的降解机制。

Abstract: Long-term, high temperature operation of Ga2O3 devices is a crucial hurdle that must be overcome before widespread adoption of the technology can be achieved, but is largely absent from the overall body of work. Demonstrations up to this point show devices are either limited by material or dopant instability that leads to performance degradation with time. Herein, Ga2O3-based hydrogen sensors employing Pt Schottky and Cr2O3/Ga2O3 p-n diodes (Mg- and N-doped) were fabricated and evaluated for long-term stability at 600C for 800-1,800 hours, with cyclic exposure to N2 and low-concentration H2 (500-1,500 ppm). Transient current density (measured at -0.1 V) and periodic J-V characterization were used to track performance. Despite gradual declines in sensor signal and sensitivity, devices distinguished hydrogen concentrations throughout weeks of operation. Degradation was architecture-dependent: Cr2O3:Mg degraded gradually, consistent with known Mg migration; the Pt Schottky diode showed dramatic changes after 1,000 hours; and Cr2O3:N showed the lowest but most stable performance before failing at 800 hours. Thermionic emission and Lambert W-based modeling confirmed hydrogen exposure reduces interfacial barrier height via a proton-induced dipole mechanism common to both diode types. TEM of aged Pt Schottky diodes revealed Pt grain growth and microvoid formation as key degradation mechanisms. TOF-SIMS confirmed nitrogen dopants remain confined to the Cr2O3:N layer, supporting N-doping as a stable, lower-performance alternative to Mg-doping
