Motivation: 物理诊断在模拟过程中产生剧烈变化的响应，但通常需要额外的计算才能将其作为代理模型的输入。本文旨在构建一种能够直接从设计变量学习物理诊断状态的方法，避免预测时的额外计算开销。

Method: 构建物理状态监督的高斯过程代理模型，采用交叉拟合的状态估计增强设计表示；全局高斯过程和可选的响应带专家混合模型使用相同输入；在一维内爆数据库上进行验证。

Result: 在1967个一维内爆案例中，从无α粒子加热的计算中学习的燃烧加权状态，使随机留出和设计空间外推的均方对数响应误差分别降低60.25%和52.98%。随机投影和打乱状态监督无法复现这些增益。在CH验证中，专家在较小训练预算下有帮助，但在1000个设计时贡献有限。热反馈基准验证了完整学习状态程序，3000个新计算的Be/Cocktail设计显示外推增益较弱。条件状态误差边界区分了编码敏感性和路由效应。

Conclusion: 物理状态监督是有限数据代理模型的有效表示方法，而专家专业化应通过验证来选择。发布了开源Python实现代码和数据。

Abstract: Physical diagnostics recorded during simulation can organize a sharply varying response, but using them as surrogate inputs normally requires another calculation at prediction. We construct a physical-state-supervised Gaussian process surrogate that learns these diagnostics from design variables. Cross-fitted state estimates augment a retained design representation; a global Gaussian process and an optional soft mixture of response-band experts use identical inputs. On a database of 1,987 paired one-dimensional implosions, learned burn-weighted states from calculations without alpha-particle heating reduce mean squared log-response error by 60.25% and 52.98% for the global predictor on random holdout and design-space extrapolation. Random-projection and shuffled-state supervision do not reproduce these gains. For CH, matched-input comparisons show that experts help at smaller training budgets but contribute little at 1,000 designs with this representation. A thermal-feedback benchmark validates the complete learned-state procedure, while 3,000 newly calculated Be/Cocktail designs expose weaker extrapolation gains and cases where experts degrade accuracy. A conditional state-error bound separates encoding sensitivity from routing effects. An open Python implementation reproduces the archived predictions and is released with the data, per-design results, and measured costs. Physical-state supervision is thus a useful representation for finite-data surrogates, whereas expert specialization should be selected by validation.
